The review in Section 3 of the manuscript is a systematic mapping review of methods: it identifies, screens and classifies published meta-learning anomaly and fault detectors on a four-dimensional taxonomy. It contains no quantitative synthesis, no effect measures and no risk-of-bias assessment, so the PRISMA 2020 items that concern those parts are marked "not applicable" below, with the reason. Items are from Page et al., BMJ 2021, 372:n71. "Location" gives the section, table or figure of the manuscript where the item is reported.
